How to play Yahtzee: rules and scoring

Goal: Make combinations with five dice to fill the 13 boxes on your score card and finish with the highest total.

Setup

You need five dice and a score card for each player. The upper section has six boxes, Ones to Sixes; the lower section has 3 of a Kind, 4 of a Kind, Full House, Small Straight, Large Straight, Yahtzee and Chance.

How to play

On your turn, roll all five dice; keep the ones you like and reroll the rest up to two more times. Then pick an empty box on your card and score it. If nothing fits, scratch a box and write 0. Each box is used once.

Scoring

Upper boxes add up the dice showing that number (three 4s = 12). 3 of a Kind, 4 of a Kind and Chance score the total of all five dice. Full House (3 + 2 alike) is 25, Small Straight (4 in a row) 30, Large Straight (5 in a row) 40, Yahtzee (5 alike) 50. An upper section of 63 or more adds a 35-point bonus.

Winning

After 13 turns everyone’s card is full; the highest total wins.

Note

In the app you enter each player’s turn on its own: pick the player, the box and the points. The next player is suggested for you.

House rules

The rules tables argue about most. Agree on them before you start; you can switch them on or off when you set up the game.

Extra Yahtzee bonus (+100)

Roll another Yahtzee while your Yahtzee box holds 50 and you get a 100-point bonus, whichever box you fill that turn.

Joker rule

If the upper box for that number is already filled, an extra Yahtzee can go in Full House, Small Straight or Large Straight for full points.
