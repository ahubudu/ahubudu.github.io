How to play Batak: rules and scoring

Goal: If you won the bid, take at least that many tricks; otherwise collect your own tricks and make the bidder fail.

Setup

A 52-card deck. With four players everyone gets 13 cards; with three, one card is removed and everyone gets 17.

How to play

Bidding comes first: players say how many tricks they will take, and the highest bidder picks trump. The bidder leads. You must follow suit and beat the trick if you can; if you can’t follow suit, you must play trump. The highest card takes the trick.

Scoring

If the bidder takes at least their bid, they score the tricks they took; otherwise they lose the bid. Everyone else scores their tricks. With the side-fail rule, a defender who takes no tricks also loses the bid.

Winning

The first to reach the target (e.g. 51) wins. With partners, players sitting opposite each other combine their tricks.
