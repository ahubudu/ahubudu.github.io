How to play 7 Wonders: rules and scoring

  • 3–7 players
  • 3 ages
  • End-of-game score

Goal: Grow your civilisation with cards over three ages and score the most points.

Setup

  • Everyone starts with a wonder board and 3 coins.

How to play

  1. In each age, pick a card to play and pass the rest on.
  2. Cards build structures, raise your wonder or earn coins.
  3. At the end of each age, compare military strength with your neighbours.

Scoring

Military± tokens
Coins3 coins = 1
Wonder, civilian, commercial, guildsas on the card
Sciencecounts squared + 7 × full sets

Winning

  • Enter the points at the end of the third age.
  • The most points wins; in a tie, the most coins.

Note

In a tie, the rules give the win to whoever has more coins.
