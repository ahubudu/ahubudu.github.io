How to play Padel: rules and scoring

Goal: Win points, games and sets, as in tennis, to take the match.

Setup

Played in pairs (four players) on a 20×10 m court enclosed by walls. A toss decides the serve and the sides.

How to play

Serve underarm, below the waist, after bouncing the ball, into the diagonal box. After bouncing once on the other side the ball may hit the walls and stay in play, and you can play balls that come back off your own walls. Hitting the ball straight into the opponents’ wall or fence loses the point.

Scoring

Points go 15, 30, 40 as in tennis. At 40-all one of the FIP’s three options is played: golden point (one deciding point), Star Point (two advantages are played, and at the third deuce one point decides) or classic advantage. Six games and two clear win a set, with a tie-break at 6-all.

Winning

The match goes to whoever wins two of three sets. Clubs often play a 10-point super tie-break instead of the third set.

Note

Variants: the golden point is the most common at amateur clubs; since 2026 professional tournaments use the Star Point too. In the app you just tap the winning pair after each point.
