How to play Backgammon: rules and scoring

Goal: Move all your checkers around the board into your home and bear them off before your opponent.

Setup

Two players, each with 15 checkers and two dice. The board has 24 points; checkers start in the standard layout.

How to play

Players take turns rolling two dice and moving checkers by the numbers rolled; doubles give four moves. You can’t land on a point held by two or more opposing checkers. Landing on a single opposing checker hits it, and a hit checker must re-enter before its owner can move anything else.

Scoring

The winner of a game scores 1 point, or 2 for a gammon (the opponent hasn’t borne off any checkers). With the doubling cube, points are multiplied by the cube value.

Winning

The first to reach the target (e.g. 5) wins the match.

Note

In Mahbusa and Gülbahar there is no hitting: in Mahbusa a checker landing on a single opposing checker pins it, and in Gülbahar both players move in the same direction.
