How to play Mini golf: rules and scoring

Goal: Finish every hole in as few strokes as you can.

Setup

Everyone has a putter and a ball in their own color. Courses usually have 9 or 18 holes. Before you start, agree on the most strokes per hole (6 at most places).

How to play

On each hole everyone putts from the tee. The player farthest from the hole goes next, or the table plays in turn. You keep putting until the ball drops. A ball that leaves the course goes back where it left, with a one-stroke penalty. Touching the obstacles (bridges, tunnels, ramps) is fine.

Scoring

Write down your strokes for every hole. A hole in one scores 1. Anyone who reaches the limit without holing out picks up and scores one more (7 with a limit of 6). Par is the expected number of strokes for a hole; finishing under par is a good result.

Winning

After the last hole, fewest strokes in total wins. For a tie, look at the last hole or count the holes in one.

Note

Variants: some courses use a limit of 5 or 7; change it on setup. With children a lower limit keeps the game moving. Teams playing “best ball” can write the team’s best score on each hole.
