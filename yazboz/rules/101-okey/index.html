How to play 101 Okey: rules and scoring

Goal: Finish the hand first, or keep your penalty points as low as possible.

Setup

The same 106 tiles and indicator as Okey. The starting player gets 22 tiles, everyone else 21.

How to play

To open, the sets you lay down must add up to at least 101, or you can open with at least five pairs. After opening you can add tiles to sets on the table. You finish by discarding your last tile.

Scoring

The finisher writes −101. Players who opened write the total of the tiles left in hand; anyone who never opened writes 202. Opening with pairs doubles your hand. Finishing with the okey or in one go doubles the penalties.

Winning

Play a set number of hands, or stop when the table decides; the lowest total wins.
