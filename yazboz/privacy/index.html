Yazboz Guru: Support & Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Support

Questions, ideas or a bug to report? Write to scoreguruapp@gmail.com, or use Settings › Contact us in the app. We read every message.

Privacy Policy

Yazboz Guru is a score-keeping app for card and board games. We built it to collect as little as possible.

Data that stays on your phone

Your games, scores, players, saved rules and history are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Tournaments you share

If you share a backgammon tournament, its name, the players' names and the scores are uploaded to our server (hosted by Supabase), so that people with the tournament's code can see it. When someone signs up for a shared tournament, the name they enter is stored there too.

  • Only people who have the tournament's code can see it. Tournaments cannot be searched or listed.
  • Only the phone that created a tournament can change it. Deleting the tournament in the app deletes it from the server.
  • We don't ask for email addresses, phone numbers, accounts or locations, and we don't link this data to you.
  • How long: finished tournaments are deleted from the server automatically 30 days after their last change, all others after 90 days. The organizer can delete a tournament in the app at any time, and people who signed up can withdraw while sign-ups are open.
  • If you see an inappropriate name, report it with "Report this tournament" on the tournament page or write to scoreguruapp@gmail.com. The organizer can remove sign-ups.
  • To prevent misuse, names pass a basic word filter and the number of tournaments and sign-ups from one network is limited. For this, the server keeps, for at most one day, a code made from the network address with a salt that changes every day, never the address itself.
  • Like any web service, the server may briefly log technical details such as IP addresses to keep the service running and secure.

Anonymous usage statistics (only if you agree)

The first time you open the app, it asks whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics. They are off unless you say yes, and you can switch them on or off any time in Settings › Privacy.

  • What is sent: which kind of game was started or finished (for example "Okey"), ranges for the number of players, rounds and minutes (such as "2–3 players"), which rule switches were turned on, the steps of the welcome tour, tournament steps (created, shared, started, finished, and whether you joined by link, QR code or typed code), use of the share buttons, a count of app errors, and technical details: app version, iOS version and the language of your device.
  • What is never sent: names of players, groups or tournaments, scores, anything you type, tournament codes, device or advertising identifiers, or your location.
  • Why: to see which games and features are used and where people get stuck, so we can improve the app. Never for ads, profiles or tracking, and never combined with other data.
  • Who processes it: Aptabase, a privacy-focused analytics service, on servers in the European Union (Aptabase acts as our processor under Article 28 GDPR). Each app session gets a random identifier that lives only in the app's memory. To count daily visitors, Aptabase derives a code from your IP address with a salt that is thrown away every 24 hours; it does not store the IP address itself.
  • How long: Aptabase keeps the statistics for up to 5 years.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Turning the switch off withdraws it; nothing is sent from then on.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data.
  • No account or login.

Contacting us

If you write to us by email, we use your message only to reply to you.

Children

Yazboz Guru is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes
